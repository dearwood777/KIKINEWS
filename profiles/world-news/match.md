# World news profile

Use this profile for major international news that a well-informed reader would want to know about: geopolitics and diplomacy, wars and conflicts, elections and political crises, major disasters and accidents, notable crime and legal cases, big infrastructure or transport events, and significant scientific or technological breakthroughs announced to the general public.

Typical items report a concrete event with a time, place, and verifiable facts: a treaty or summit outcome, an election result, a ceasefire, a court ruling, a large-scale accident, a breakthrough discovery, or a company/state decision with broad public consequence.

Do not use this profile for narrow technical releases, product marketing, market-by-market finance coverage, celebrity gossip, pure opinion pieces, or long-form essays without a news event.
