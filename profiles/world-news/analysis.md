# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much a general but curious reader needs to know about this international news event today.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Globally significant.** Events with wide and lasting consequences: war escalation, major election outcomes, state-level policy shifts, disasters with heavy casualties, landmark legal rulings, breakthroughs that change what is possible.
- **7-8: Important.** Events that matter beyond one country and are likely to be discussed for days: notable court verdicts, big accidents, high-level diplomatic moves, significant scientific or technological firsts.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** Regional events with clear public impact, notable crime cases, unusual but real developments.
- **3-4: Minor.** Local incidents, routine political statements, small-scale accidents.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumours, unverified claims, celebrity items, promotional or opinion-only content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward concrete facts: who, where, when, numbers, official confirmations, and named institutions. Distinguish confirmed reporting from single-source claims. Do not equate drama with importance and do not reward clickbait framing. Prefer items with a clear event over general commentary.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
