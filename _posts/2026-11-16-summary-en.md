The stolen keys were validated and added to a shared pool for further large‑model usage. Amazon’s MadPot honeypot intercepted and blocked the activity on Nov 5, after it began on Oct 3. Amazon attributes the operation to a Chinese threat group, notes that not all listed targets were compromised, and has notified potentially affected AWS customers.

It was first released by BaaRai and has been used in prior attacks, such as the Storm‑0940 supply‑chain compromise that abused Microsoft 365 credentials.

**「Impact」** The incident exposes that publicly exposed AI service keys can be harvested and reused for large‑model calls, potentially incurring unauthorized usage and costs for affected AWS customers, who should audit key exposure and enable monitoring.
