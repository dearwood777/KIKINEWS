US Loses Measles-Free Status After 26 Years▍8.0/10

rss · BBC World · Nov 17, 21:04

The United States lost its measles‑free status on 17 November 2026, after the Pan American Health Organization confirmed uninterrupted transmission of a single measles strain for over 12 months. The CDC reported 5,400 cases, the highest in recent history, and said it would work to regain elimination status. Mexico and Bolivia also lost elimination status, attributed to vaccination gaps.

Background: Measles elimination criteria

Measles was declared eliminated in the United States in 2000, a status that the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) monitors. PAHO defines elimination as no uninterrupted transmission of the same measles strain for at least 12 months; if this criterion is breached, a country loses its elimination status, and PAHO will evaluate the U.S. status in November 2026.

Impact

The loss means the U.S. must increase vaccination coverage and surveillance, potentially leading to higher outbreak risk and strain on public health resources.
